Architecture and ISO practices

The repository shows several practices that support ISO alignment. Formal conformance and certification would require an assessment of the complete architecture documentation, production controls, and organizational records.

How requests flow

  1. Users sign in through Django and select Policy AI or Agentic AI from the app menu.
  2. Policy AI checks the user's app permission before sending authorized questions to the configured policy search service.
  3. Agentic AI checks tool permissions, data scope, and any required approval before calling the configured gateway or application tools.
  4. Reporting uses role and delivery permissions to control publishing or sharing. Delivery also requires a configured provider and approved recipient.

Practices and evidence to verify

  • Architecture description. The repo documents individual integrations and deployment procedures. The review still needs a consolidated set of views covering system boundaries, data flows, deployment, stakeholders, and significant design decisions.
  • Identity and least privilege. App grants, tool permissions, branch-level access, and audited changes are implemented. Verify how production owners review role membership and remove access when employees change roles or leave.
  • Secrets and production configuration. The production settings load secrets from the environment. Verify the deployed settings, transport security at each connection, and records of credential rotation.
  • Data protection. Report access and delivery recipients are restricted. Document which personal and business data reaches databases, logs, AI providers, and delivery providers, then record its retention and deletion requirements.
  • Audit and incident response. The app records access changes and report activity. Verify who can read the logs, how long they are retained, who responds to alerts, and when the response process was exercised.
  • Recovery and change control. The repo includes a SQLite backup recovery runbook and deployment procedures. Verify restore drill results, recovery targets, and release approvals for the deployed environment.
  • AI governance. Tool authorization and approval checks provide oversight for sensitive actions. Record AI system owners, intended uses, and risks. Verify evaluation evidence for policy-answer accuracy, unauthorized data disclosure, and the effects of model or prompt changes.
