Access rules

No access is the default, including for administrators until an explicit grant is saved.

Selected scope combines chosen branches with every active branch in the chosen zones.

All access includes current and future active branches in this directory. A separate permission is required to grant it.

Paused branches, paused zones and expired policies cannot be queried. A query cannot extend a user's saved scope.
